Historical cost evidence is a separate version: the pre-B07/C04 benchmark on 512 sampled directional queries, three base seeds, five measured repetitions each, with three warm-ups and an A100. Copy the returned raw/summary/seed/hardware/manifest files into an immutable small snapshot, validate recorded output hashes and reproduce summary means/SDs from all 240 raw observations. Ratios are ratios of directly measured mean wall times, not the reported relative-overhead field and not sums of stage means. Selector-stage overhead means feature extraction plus policy inference plus combination/ranking. It is neither a pure neural forward pass nor production response latency. The historical Dyna selector is not the healthy R3 control. Exact executed code revision was not recorded in this benchmark manifest.

Historical timing must not be attached to corrected MRR as a contemporaneous efficiency frontier. Report its adverse cost as historical evidence, with current timing unavailable. Fourteen trainable logistic parameters exclude preprocessing state, both frozen scorers and candidate-distribution processing. Fallback happens after scoring; it cannot save the secondary forward pass in this implementation. Whether small, heterogeneous gains justify cost is deployment-dependent and not established by this study. A current performance claim would require a separately versioned corrected benchmark on the server; no such claim is made here.
